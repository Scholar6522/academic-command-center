# No-Retake Readiness Protocol

The calendar says when an exam is available. Practice evidence decides whether you are ready.

## GED

Target: 175–200 in every subject.

Gate:
- Official diagnostic completed for all four subjects.
- Weak domains identified and repaired.
- Realistic practice is consistently at or above the target range.
- Timing is comfortable.

## ACT

Target: 36 Composite; Science target 35–36 if taken.

Gate:
- Multiple full-length timed practice tests.
- Composite consistently 35–36.
- No section with an unstable score.
- Repeated errors have been eliminated from the error log.

## SAT

Target: 1550–1600.

Gate:
- Multiple fresh Bluebook adaptive tests.
- Scores repeatedly in the target range.
- Weak domains are no longer recurring sources of missed points.
- Test-day timing and device setup have been rehearsed.

## AP

Target: 5.

Gate:
- Full syllabus completed.
- Timed AP-style sets consistently at 5-level.
- At least two realistic full-exam rehearsals.

## Cambridge

Target: A/A*.

Gate:
- Exact exam syllabus checked line-by-line.
- Multiple fresh timed past papers completed.
- Mark schemes reviewed after each paper.
- Examiner reports used to eliminate recurring mark-loss patterns.
- Target-grade performance is consistent, not a one-off.
